About me

I’m a multidisciplinary designer with experience in both graphic design and UI/UX, creating visuals that combine aesthetics, strategy, and business goals. My work spans digital and print design — from advertising campaigns, social media assets, and branded merchandise to landing pages, websites, and large-scale outdoor visuals, including Times Square billboards.

With a background in Information Technologies and hands-on experience in marketing design, I focus on building clear, impactful, and visually strong solutions that help brands grow across digital and offline platforms. I collaborate closely with marketing and SMM teams, developing performance-driven creatives and maintaining consistent brand identity across multiple touchpoints.

I actively integrate AI tools into the design process — from research and ideation to image generation and production — allowing me to create modern, bold, and efficient creative solutions.
